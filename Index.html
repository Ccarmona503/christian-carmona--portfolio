About Me

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Landscape Architecture from the University of Connecticut and am currently pursuing a Master of Architecture at Northeastern University.


My work explores how architecture can strengthen the relationship between people, place, and landscape. I am particularly interested in residential design, sustainability, and the craft of creating spaces that remain meaningful over time.
